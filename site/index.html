How it works

1. Silent by default

It reads the page on your device and says nothing unless it clearly looks like a scam. A false alarm is worse than a miss.

2. Explains in plain words

It names the scam, marks the exact scary phrases, and says what usually happens next and what to do now.

3. Pauses the payment

Only on a scam page, the Pay button waits. You can cancel, ask family on WhatsApp, or continue. Continue always works.

Covers digital-arrest threats, fake KYC and bank links, "safe account" requests, OTP theft, fake parcel fees, and scams aimed at shops: fake supplier bank changes, "payment done" tricks, and QR "receive money" tricks. English, Hinglish and Hindi text is understood. The screens are available in English, Hindi and Tamil.

Get Clippit

Browser extension

The only version that can pause a payment.

  1. Download the extension folder from the project.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the folder.

Android app

Share any suspicious message to Clippit, or select text and choose Clippit. It reads warnings aloud and has no internet permission.

Build it with node mobile/build.js, or install the APK from the project's releases page if one is published.

This web page on a phone

Open this page in Chrome on Android and choose "Install app". Then share a message to it.

It only checks messages you share. It cannot read your messages or stop a payment. Sharing works best on Android; iPhone support is limited.

Honest limits

  • It cannot hear phone calls.
  • New wording can fool the rules, and spelling in Hinglish varies a lot.
  • It was tested on small, hand-written sets of messages, not real traffic, so the numbers are only a guide.
  • "No signals found" does not prove a message is safe. If in doubt, call your bank on the number printed on your card.
  • Explanations are still English in the Hindi and Tamil screens. The Hindi and Tamil text should be reviewed by native speakers.

If money was lost, call 1930 (India's national cybercrime helpline) right away.
