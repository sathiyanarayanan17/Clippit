Get Clippit

On a computer: the browser extension

It stays quiet until a page looks like a scam. Then it pauses the Pay button before money moves.

  1. Download the extension folder from the project.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the folder.

On a phone: add this page to your home screen

On Android, open this page in Chrome and choose "Install app". Then, on any suspicious SMS or WhatsApp message, tap Share and pick Clippit. It checks the message at once.

The phone version only checks messages you share. It cannot read your messages or stop a payment. The extension is the only part that pauses a payment. Sharing to an installed web app works best on Android; support on iPhone is limited.

How it works

  • About 30 signals, such as fake authority, urgency, secrecy, OTP requests and odd links.
  • It understands warnings. "Never share your OTP" from a real bank is not flagged.
  • Everything runs on your device. No account, no server, no tracking.

Honest limits

  • It cannot hear phone calls.
  • New wording can fool the rules.
  • It was tested on a small set of hand-written messages, not real traffic.
  • "No signals found" does not prove a message is safe. If in doubt, call your bank on the number printed on your card.

If money was lost, call 1930 (India's national cybercrime helpline) right away.
